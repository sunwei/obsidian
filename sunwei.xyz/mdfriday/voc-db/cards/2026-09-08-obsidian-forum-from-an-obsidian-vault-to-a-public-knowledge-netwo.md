# From an Obsidian vault to a public knowledge network: my current approach

> I have kept personal notes in Markdown and Obsidian for years. As LLMs made knowledge easier to obtain, I started wondering whether maintaining those notes—and publishing a blog—still had value. My current answer is that knowledge is not only a collection of documents. It is also the network between ideas: how they support, limit, and challenge one another, and how new material changes an earlier understanding. I then made the public part of this real knowledge network visible as a blog called See the Forest. Its homepage is a constellation of real topics and articles rather than a list of recent posts. This is not an Obsidian plugin or a downloadable template. I am sharing the PKM approach I use and the public result built from it.
