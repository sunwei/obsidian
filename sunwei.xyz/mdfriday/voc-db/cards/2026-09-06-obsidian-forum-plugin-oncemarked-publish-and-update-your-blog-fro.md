# [Plugin] OnceMarked — publish and update your blog from Obsidian

> When I tried to make my own blog (yes, in 2026, the age of AI, even the most rebellious teenagers would think twice), I surveyed all the good options on the market, but none quite meets my need. For me, it needs to have the following features: The blog posts need to be really fast to load, nice looking, and reads well on mobile. The editing experience should be as simple as iOS Notes app, or Obsidian. I can write and publish easily, and make changes to published posts. I like writing in markdown, so it should support that. Great themes, and allow me to easily customise it through CSS. Adding images should be seamless, and compress for me so they load fast. It needs to have a natural discovery layer, and comments. Out of box RSS and SEO basics. So as any regular, sane person would do, I built my own: OnceMarked Blog… most importantly, it has a free, open-source companion plugin for Obsidian, which follows the Micropub convention and allows me to publish my Obsidian contents straight to my blog, and update the same post when I make changes.
